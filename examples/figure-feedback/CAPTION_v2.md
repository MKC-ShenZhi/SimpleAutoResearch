# Figure caption — version 2

**Chronological benzene calibration: roles and visualized saved errors.** Left to right: (A) historical sensor inputs and reference C6H6(GT) labels fit and validate linear and quadratic Ridge models; validation selects α = 0.1 for both, with no later retuning. (B) prediction-time inputs are five sensor responses plus temperature, relative humidity (RH), and absolute humidity (AH), passed through the fitted transformations and coefficients; current-month reference labels are not prediction inputs. Frozen coefficients remain fixed after 31 October 2004. Monthly refits use an expanding history and require labels strictly earlier than each month boundary. (C) C6H6(GT) evaluation labels score the same 3,493 eligible hours from 1 November 2004 to 4 April 2005, 14:00. Earlier evaluation labels subsequently enter later monthly refits; this is therefore not a permanently label-isolated test.

(D) Overall saved errors are shown as paired visual comparisons: an open circle is Frozen and a filled square is Monthly; the connecting segment makes the within-family policy contrast visual for both MAE and RMSE. All eight recorded values are printed to four decimals in µg/m³. Linear and quadratic Ridge use separate zero-based linear scales (0–3 and 0–0.18 µg/m³, respectively), explicitly labeled because their magnitudes differ; values are not compared by horizontal position across families. Lower is better. Solid panels denote fitting/prediction; the dashed evaluation and error panel denotes scoring/reporting, not reference-label input to inference.

**Scope.** A single archive contains dependent hours. Complete-case selection excludes 218 of 3,711 evaluation hours (−200 / blanks; no imputation). November predictions are identical across policies. No external-site performance, drift causality, target independence, metrological validity, or deployment readiness is established; label acquisition, delays, quality control, and rollback were not evaluated. Quadratic predictions include negatives (9 frozen, 6 monthly); overall improvements need not hold monthly (December quadratic RMSE: 0.1263 frozen, 0.1304 updated). This revision uses only the supplied saved results; it does not refit models or estimate new metrics.
